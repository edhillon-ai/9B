# In This Town — Everyone Is Unemployed

- `In_This_Town_Everyone_Is_Unemployed.mp4` — 90.0 s, 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, H.264 + AAC.
- No captions, subtitles or watermarks (only in-world signs/screens such as the bakery sign, phone and admin monitors).

- Rendered at 1080p, not 4K (CPU-only renderer).
